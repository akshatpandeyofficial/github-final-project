# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers of the Simple Interest Calculator
project, pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open,
welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

-   Using welcoming and inclusive language.
-   Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.
-   Accepting constructive criticism gracefully.
-   Focusing on what is best for the community.
-   Showing empathy toward other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, insults, personal
attacks, discriminatory language, and publishing others' private
information without permission.

## Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our
standards of acceptable behavior. They may take appropriate and fair
corrective action in response to behavior they consider inappropriate,
threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and when an
individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for enforcing the standards of this
Code of Conduct. They may remove, edit, or reject contributions and
comments that violate it, and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation for behavior they deem inappropriate.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will follow these guidelines when addressing incidents:

1.  **Correction:** For a minor first violation, issue a private or
    public warning and explain the nature of the violation.
2.  **Warning:** For a repeated or more serious violation, issue a
    warning with consequences for continued behavior.
3.  **Temporary Ban:** For repeated violations or serious misconduct,
    temporarily restrict participation.
4.  **Permanent Ban:** For a pattern of misconduct or a serious
    violation, permanently remove participation privileges.

Maintainers will be respectful and fair when reviewing reports and
determining consequences.
